The story behind imaGine

The canvas
is air.

imaGine turns the space around you into a place to think, sketch, and share— without asking you to reach for a pen or paper.

01 / Vision

Make ideas feel immediate.

We believe the best tools get out of the way of the person using them. imaGine is a hands-free canvas for thoughts that arrive faster than they can be typed—an open surface where a gesture can become a line, a note, or a shared explanation.

02 / Motivation

Explain it as you see it.

Ideas are often easier to understand when they are drawn. imaGine was created to help teachers, presenters, students, and teams capture those visual moments naturally, then keep them as a note they can export and share.
